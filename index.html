We believe you don't have to do something huge to make a difference. Sometimes showing up, helping someone in need, or sharing a little kindness is enough to brighten someone's day.

This Little Light Project

Turning good intentions into action.

This Little Light Project was created with a simple idea: look for ways to help, then do something about it.

Through community projects, collections, acts of service and giving, our goal is to help people who could use a little extra light in their lives.

Every project may look a little different, but the purpose remains the same — find a need, lend a hand and encourage others to do the same.
